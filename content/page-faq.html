About the drops

What signs suggest my dog could use a parasite cleanse?

Many signs are easy to miss because they look like everyday habits: scooting or bottom-dragging, constant paw licking, itchy or flaky skin, loose stools or occasional vomiting, a dull coat, low energy or restless nights. You won’t always see worms — many parasites are too small to notice. These signs can also have other causes, so if they persist, check in with your vet.

How is this different from a chemical dewormer?

Harsh dewormers usually target adult worms and stop there — they don’t support the gut afterwards. PawGuard combines natural botanicals that support the body in addressing parasites at every life stage (eggs, larvae and adults) with a probiotic blend that helps restore healthy gut flora. Gentle enough for daily use.

Will my dog pass visible worms?

Sometimes, but often not — and that’s normal. Most parasites are microscopic. Rather than a one-off purge, PawGuard supports your dog’s gut steadily over the full 90-day routine.

My dog tested negative for parasites. Is this still worth it?

It can be. A single stool test only captures one moment and doesn’t always catch everything, and daily walks, parks and other dogs mean exposure is ongoing. Supporting the gut every day helps your dog stay resilient either way. Your vet can advise on what’s right for your dog.

How soon will I see results?

Many owners notice changes within the first couple of weeks — firmer stools, less scooting, calmer nights. Paw licking often eases after that. Gut health builds with daily use, so give it the full 90 days for the most noticeable results. Every dog is different.

Using the drops

How much do I give my dog?

Once a day, added to food, based on your dog’s weight:

  • Under 10 lbs — 0.5 ml
  • 10–35 lbs — 1 ml
  • 35–75 lbs — 2 ml
  • Over 75 lbs — 3 ml

Each 60 ml bottle lasts about 60 days for a 10–35 lb dog, and less for larger dogs. A full dosing guide comes with every bottle.

Can I use it alongside other supplements?

Yes. The drops are a daily food topper and can be given with other supplements. If your dog takes prescription medication, check with your vet before adding anything new.

Is it suitable for puppies, seniors or pregnant dogs?

It’s suitable for dogs from 12 weeks old, seniors included. It isn’t recommended for pregnant or nursing dogs, or for dogs with a tree-nut allergy, because the formula contains black walnut. If your dog has a health condition or takes medication, ask your vet first.
